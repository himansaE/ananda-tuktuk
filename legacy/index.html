Travel questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I visit from Embul Ambe?

You can visit Dambulla Cave Temple, Sigiriya Lion Rock, Pidurangala Rock, Ibbankatuwa, village lakes, Popham's Arboretum, and safari meeting points for Minneriya or Kaudulla.

How do I book a tuk-tuk tour?

Send a WhatsApp message with your travel date, pickup place, start time, and the places you want to visit. The route and quote are confirmed privately.

Can you pick me up from Dambulla or Sigiriya?

Yes. Pickup can usually be arranged from Embul Ambe, Dambulla, Sigiriya, nearby guesthouses, hotels, and Kurunegala-Dambulla road bus stops.

Are entrance tickets included?

Entrance tickets, safari jeep fees, meals, and guide fees are separate unless agreed in advance on WhatsApp.
